# Route Page Copy Guidelines

This document defines copy constraints for inner route pages such as `Compare`, `Explore`, `Registry`, `Regulation`, `Methodology`, and company/detail flows.

## Goal

Route pages should feel operational, neutral, and task-oriented.

Landing-page style framing can exist on the homepage, but inner pages should avoid sounding promotional, self-congratulatory, or overly interpretive.

## Avoided Language

On route pages, reduce or avoid the following phrases and patterns where possible:

- `by design`
- `truth`
- `serious platform`
- `decision clarity`
- `expert-depth`
- `refuses`
- `protects integrity`
- `heavy landing flow`
- `the product`
- `the interface`

These phrases often read like marketing language or internal design language instead of clear user-facing operational copy.

## Preferred Direction

Prefer copy that answers:

- What can the user do here?
- What will they see?
- What boundary or limitation applies?

Good route-page copy is usually:

- task-based
- neutral
- specific
- short

## Example Rewrite Direction

Instead of:

- `Comparison is conditional by design`

Prefer:

- `Check whether two values are comparable`

Instead of:

- `The interface refuses winner language and explains the method boundary instead.`

Prefer:

- `Comparison results are shown with method boundaries instead of winner labels.`
